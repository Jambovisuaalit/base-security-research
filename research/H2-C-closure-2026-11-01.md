## Local-fork test results
- Unprivileged BaseFeeVault `withdraw()` receipt: **success** (`status=1`).
- Initial vault balance and withdrawal: **5,458,957,002,828,359,002 wei**.
- FeeDisburser balance increase: **5,458,957,002,828,359,002 wei**.
- `totalProcessed` increase: **5,458,957,002,828,359,002 wei**, once.
- Vault residual: **252,198,196,200 wei**, consistent with fresh L2 transaction fee accrual (transaction fee: **271,067,045,250 wei**).
- No ETH recycling was observed.

## Threat model and conclusion
The earlier test-only H2-C demonstration required a malicious, attacker-controlled recipient to return ETH and reenter `withdraw()`. That recipient is not configured in the pinned mainnet state. The main vaults' recipient settings are immutable in their current implementations, and the actual FeeDisburser receive path does not reenter. No permissionless route to attacker-controlled recipient or measurable financial loss was established.

**Disposition: NO-GO.** Do not characterize gross `totalProcessed` accounting behavior in the privileged/malicious-recipient test as a demonstrated mainnet exploit. Reopen only if a new deployment/configuration, a permissionless path, or verified economic impact materially changes the evidence.
